Hi, I'm Rahul. I'm a data engineer based in New Jersey with five-plus years of building pipelines for tech and finance teams. Right now I'm at Meta, and before that I was at Bank of America and Fidelity.

Most of my work sits in the middle of the data stack. Events come in through Kafka, get cleaned up and reshaped in PySpark, land in Delta Lake or Snowflake, and get modeled in dbt so analysts can trust the numbers. I also write the checks that catch schema drift and bad data before anyone downstream sees it, and I spend a fair bit of time making slow Spark jobs faster.

I'm open to my next senior data engineer role anywhere in the US: remote, hybrid, or on-site, contract or full-time.
